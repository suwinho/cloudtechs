=== 1. Dodanie produktu przed restartem ===
$ curl -X POST http://localhost/api/items -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"name": "Trwaly_Produkt", "price": 100}'
{"id": 4, "name": "Trwaly_Produkt", "price": "100.00"}

=== 2. Sprawdzenie stanu bazy przed restartem ===
$ curl -s http://localhost/api/items
[{"id":1,"name":"Sample Product 1","price":"19.99"},{"id":2,"name":"Sample Product 2","price":"29.99"},{"id":3,"name":"Test Product","price":"99.99"},{"id":4,"name":"Trwaly_Produkt","price":"100.00"}]

=== 3. Restart kontenera bazy PostgreSQL ===
$ docker restart postgres-db
postgres-db
